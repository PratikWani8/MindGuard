# MindGuard Wellness Knowledge Base

This starter knowledge-base contains general educational guidance only.

## Stress management
Short recovery breaks, paced breathing, grounding activities, realistic task planning, movement, and talking with trusted people can be useful general wellness strategies.

## Sleep
A consistent sleep and wake schedule, a relaxing wind-down routine, and reducing stimulating activities near bedtime can support healthy sleep habits.

## When to seek professional support
Persistent or worsening emotional distress can be a reason to speak with a qualified mental-health professional. MindGuard is not a substitute for professional assessment or emergency care.
